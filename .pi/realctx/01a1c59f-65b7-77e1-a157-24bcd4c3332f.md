Live `AcpDriver` probes against agy 1.5.3 show thinking chunk granularity is model-dependent.

`gemini-3.8-flash-high`: three thought activities (roughly 200–400 characters each) at 12.4s, 13.9s, and 20.1s; answer text then arrived in small chunks every ~50–100ms. `src/acp/driver.ts` forwards each thought immediately after the prompt echo, and `src/provider.ts` maps it directly to `thinking_delta`. Thus the non-typewriter thinking appearance for this model originates in the upstream chunk granularity; smoothing it would be artificial and may delay subsequent text/tool events.

`claude-sonnet-5-5-high`: thought chunks of a few characters every ~30 ms initially, then some frames arriving in a burst. The driver passes individual thought frames through; unlike Gemini's paragraph-sized thought summaries, this model can produce typewriter-like incremental thinking.
