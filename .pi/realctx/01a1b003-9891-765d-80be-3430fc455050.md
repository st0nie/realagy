Instead of omitting the middle at 160 KB, `src/transcript.ts` `transcriptInput()` serializes `pi://transcript` / `pi://transcript-delta` with `serializeBounded(..., MAX_PARTED_BYTES ≈ 8×90%×160 KB)` and splits the text with `splitUtf8()` (≤ `MAX_TRANSCRIPT_BYTES`, cut at a newline in the last 10% or at a UTF-8 boundary). Earlier pieces go in `AcpInput.parts` / `DriverTurnRequest.parts` as `{prompt: transcriptPartPrompt(i,n), contextBlock}`; the final prompt carries the last piece plus a "part n of n" note. `AcpDriver#deliverParts` sends each part as its own `session/prompt` with `turn.priming = true` (all frames except usage_update dropped; `driver.priming` makes `ToolRoundTrips.onToolCall` refuse pi tools). Main use: switching to an agy model mid-session with no checkpoint (fresh) or after another model's long run (big delta). Live 2026-11-06 with gemini-3.8-flash: ~560 KB → 3 parts + final; model recalled a fact only present in part 1, acks did not leak into pi's reply. Note: `serializeBounded` drops a single message bigger than the whole budget (unless it is the last), it does not split it.
