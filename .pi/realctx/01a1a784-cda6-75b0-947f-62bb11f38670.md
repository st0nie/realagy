caption: Pi session replacement invalidates background extension contexts
paths:
  - extensions/index.ts
created: 2026-11-04T17:04:20.902Z
updated: 2026-11-04T17:04:20.902Z
---

Pi emits session_shutdown and replaces the extension runtime for /resume, /new, /fork and /reload. Background catalog refresh and auth callbacks in extensions/index.ts must check the shutdown flag after awaits and before accessing ctx or pi; otherwise stale context access can crash pi.
