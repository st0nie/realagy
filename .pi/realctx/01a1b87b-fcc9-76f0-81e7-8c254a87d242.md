`DEFAULT_RECLAIM_POLICY` (`src/transcript.ts`) and `DEFAULT_RECLAIM_SETTINGS` (`src/settings.ts`) default to `reclaimMinTokens: 8_000` (`minBytes: 32_000`), `reclaimMinPercent: 50` (`minShare: 0.5`), and `reclaimColdMinutes: 5` (`coldMs: 300_000`). UI presets: `["0%", "10%", "20%", "30%", "50%", "75%"]`.

While the prompt cache is warm (`cold === false`), both between-turn `applyReclaim` and mid-turn `measureWarm` require reclaiming ≥50% of `warm.bytes` and ≥8,000 tokens before restarting on a fresh session. This preserves the 10x cached-prefix discount for moderate drops/summaries. Once `TranscriptIndex.cold(policy.coldMs)` indicates ≥5 minutes idle, ≥8,000 reclaimed tokens alone triggers a fresh session regardless of share.

Fresh sessions resend the surviving transcript uncached. An observed mid-session summary restart billed `input=30,976` versus ~3k–7k on warm turns; upstream full cache misses were observed after 302–482s idle gaps.
