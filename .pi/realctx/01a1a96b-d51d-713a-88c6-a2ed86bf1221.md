agy_acp_server >= 1.3.0 emits `session/update` `usage_update` {used,size} (context occupancy, size >= 1M), reportedly once per prompt turn, not per internal model call. PromptResponse.usage is absent. Exact per-model-call tokens are only in the server's SQLite store `~/.gemini/antigravity-acp/conversations/<sessionId>.db`: `SELECT metadata FROM steps WHERE step_type IN (15, 2) AND metadata IS NOT NULL ORDER BY idx DESC`; protobuf field 9 holds varints 2=promptTokens (uncached), 3=candidateTokens, 5=cachedTokens (source: simonepri/refined-antigravity-acp src/otel/tracker.ts). Newer servers may also put `_meta.quota.token_count.{input_tokens,output_tokens}` on PromptResponse (zacbemis/pi-antigravity-acp-provider src/stream/usage.ts).
